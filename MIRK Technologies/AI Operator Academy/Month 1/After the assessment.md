
What happens after the assessment?

After the assessment, we analyze the information and determine where quick wins and potential for longer term projects exist and identify the tools that will help get you there. Then we create a report and review it with you. At this point, you can take the report and do whatever you would like with it; diy the solution or have us or someone else implement it for you. We will follow up in 2 weeks and 1 month and see how things are and try a retainer offer.
