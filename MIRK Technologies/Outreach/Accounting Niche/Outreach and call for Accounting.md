# MIRK Accounting Outreach

## Outreach message
Option 1: 
Hi [Name], I'm Ian, a local software engineer in Mesa who builds AI tools for small businesses. I'm researching how small accounting firms in the East Valley spend their time outside of tax season, things like chasing clients for documents and answering the same questions over and over. Could I ask you three or four quick questions over a 15-minute call? I'm not selling anything, just learning. Happy to share what I hear from other firms afterward.

Option 2:
Hi [name], I'm Ian, a local software engineer in Mesa who builds AI tools for small businesses. I'm researching how small accounting firms in the East Valley spend their time outside of tax season, things like chasing clients for documents and answering the same questions over and over. 

Given your firm's solid experience in the space, I would value your perspective. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call for me to ask three or four brief questions? I am not selling anything, just gathering insights, and I'm happy to share what I learn from other local firms afterward.

Add one specific line per firm (something noticed on their site or reviews).

## Target criteria
- 2-15 people, owner still makes decisions
- Signs of busyness: recent hiring, many reviews, bookkeeping or advisory beyond tax prep
- Friction to spot: phone-only contact, no online booking
- Skip: franchises, seasonal tax-prep storefronts

## Call questions
1. Walk me through a typical week outside of tax season. Where does your time actually go?
2. What's the most annoying, repetitive thing your staff handles?
3. How do you currently get documents from clients, and how often do you have to chase them?
4. What happens when someone calls and nobody can answer? Do you lose leads?
5. Have you tried any software or AI tools? What worked and what didn't?
6. If you could wave a wand and fix one thing in your firm, what would it be?
7. How do new clients usually find you, and what happens between their first inquiry and signing on?

Best: #6, plus "tell me more about that." Ask what each pain costs in hours or lost clients. Don't pitch on the call.

## Weekly target
5 messages a day, 25 total by Friday.
