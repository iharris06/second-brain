## The NotebookLM Audit Workflow
### Step 1: Feed the Notebook (The Data Dump)
Once the 15 employees submit their GHL surveys (using voice-to-text), you export their responses.
 * Create a new notebook in NotebookLM titled **"[Client Name] Operational Audit"**
 * Drop each employee's survey in as a separate source document (labeled by their role, e.g., Sarah_Ops_Manager.txt, John_Commercial_Estimator.txt).
 * You can also upload the company's actual SOPs or a transcript of your 1-on-1 call with the owner.
### Step 2: Leverage the "Source-Grounded" Intelligence
Unlike standard ChatGPT or Claude which relies on general knowledge, NotebookLM **only** answers based on the documents you uploaded. It creates an instant semantic index of the entire company's messy human workflows.
You can then use the internal chat feature to interrogate the company's data.
**Pro-Tip Prompts to type into your Notebook:**
 * *"Cross-reference all 15 sources. What is the single most commonly cited software tool causing frustration or lag time across departments?"*

NotebookLM has a feature called **"Studio"** or **"Briefing Docs"** that will automatically synthesize your sources into high-level summaries, timelines, or FAQs.
