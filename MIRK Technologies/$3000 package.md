## Deliverable 2: The Team-Wide Role Efficiency Survey
*Build this as an open-ended survey in GHL. Add a note at the top encouraging them to use **Voice-to-Text (Dictation)** on their phones/computers so they can vent freely and give Claude maximum data to crunch.*
### Section 1: The Tool Stack Audit
 * **Q1: List every software tool, portal, CRM, or shared spreadsheet you use to do your job.** *(Example: HubSpot, Excel, QuickBooks, internal company portal).*
 * **Q2: Which software tool causes you the most frustration, glitches, or lag time, and why?**
### Section 2: Administrative Drudgery & Data Movement
 * **Q3: Describe any task where you are manually copy-pasting, downloading/uploading files, or re-typing info from one system to another.** *(Be specific: e.g., 'I copy invoice numbers from email into our tracking sheet').*
 * **Q4: How much of your day (roughly in hours or percentage) is spent on purely administrative data entry vs. actually executing your core skill?**
### Section 3: Information Silos & Delays
 * **Q5: When you need to find an internal company policy, pricing structure, or standard procedure, where do you look, and how long does it take you to find it?**
 * **Q6: What is the most common question or piece of information you have to ask a coworker or manager for because it isn't easily searchable?**
 * **Q7: Describe a time this week when you were stuck waiting on a client, vendor, or coworker to give you information before you could proceed with your work. What were you waiting for?**
### Section 4: Process Mechanics & Rules
 * **Q8: Think of a complex, multi-step process you handle regularly. What are the specific 'if/then' rules you follow to complete it?** *(e.g., 'If the client is commercial, I route to Bill. If residential, I route to Sarah').*
 * **Q9: If we could build a custom digital assistant that perfectly handled just ONE of your repetitive tasks, what would you hand off to it today?**
 * **Q10: Use this space to describe any other bottleneck, sloppy process, or operational headache you notice in your department that could be streamlined.**
