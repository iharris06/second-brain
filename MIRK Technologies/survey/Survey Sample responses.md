### Test Case 1: The E-Commerce Brand (High Volume, Simple Stack)

_Use this to test how the system handles short, punchy answers with well-known retail platforms._

- **Industry:** E-commerce & Direct-to-Consumer Apparel
    
- **Business Size:** 8 employees / $2.4M annual revenue
    
- **Ecosystem Platforms Q1:** Shopify, Klaviyo, Loop Returns, and QuickBooks Online.
    
- **Admin Friction & Risk Q2:** Manually logging return reasons from Loop into a spreadsheet to track defective inventory. If it’s delayed, we overorder bad stock from our manufacturer, costing us thousands in dead inventory.
    
- **Primary Bottleneck & Hours Q3:** Reconciling daily Shopify payouts with QuickBooks bank feeds. The team wastes about 8 hours a week hunting down missing transaction fees.
    
- **Knowledge Base State Q4:** A messy mix of shared Google Docs, pinned Slack messages, and unwritten rules in our head customer support agent's mind.
    
- **Information Synthesis Tax Q5:** When a customer asks about a delayed or lost custom shipment, support has to manually check Shopify, cross-reference the tracking link on the UPS portal, and then look inside a shared Google Sheet of backordered items.
    
- **Conditional Logic Rules Q6:** When processing a high-value VIP customer refund: If the order is over $200 and the customer has been with us for over a year, approve an instant cash refund plus a 15% bonus gift card. Otherwise, offer standard store credit.
    
- **Multi-App Loop Platforms Q7:** Yes: Shopify, Loop Returns, UPS Shipping Portal, and Gorgias Support Helpdesk.
    

### Test Case 2: The B2B Service Provider (Complex Logic, Enterprise Leanings)

_Use this to test long-form text payloads and advanced conditional logic formatting._

- **Industry:** Commercial Commercial HVAC & Facilities Management
    
- **Business Size:** 45 employees / $12M annual revenue
    
- **Ecosystem Platforms Q1:** HubSpot CRM, ServiceTitan, FieldEdge, and legacy Sage 300 accounting software.
    
- **Admin Friction & Risk Q2:** Transferring closed-won deal details from HubSpot into ServiceTitan to create a new commercial work order. If it's delayed or typed wrong, technicians show up to a job site with the wrong equipment, causing major client friction.
    
- **Primary Bottleneck & Hours Q3:** Waiting on on-site technicians to submit compliance checklists before invoicing can happen. The office team wastes 15 to 20 hours a week chasing guys down for paperwork.
    
- **Knowledge Base State Q4:** Hundreds of technical equipment manuals and client service level agreements (SLAs) saved as static PDFs across a confusing corporate OneDrive folder.
    
- **Information Synthesis Tax Q5:** When a technician is on a roof trying to figure out if a specific chiller unit is still covered under a custom client SLA, the dispatcher has to search through physical contract PDFs in OneDrive to find the exact warranty clause.
    
- **Conditional Logic Rules Q6:** For emergency dispatch dispatching: If a priority client submits a request and their contract SLA guarantees a 2-hour response window, assign the nearest Tier 3 senior technician. If no senior technician is within a 15-mile radius, automatically route it to the on-call supervisor for approval.
    
- **Multi-App Loop Platforms Q7:** Yes: HubSpot CRM, ServiceTitan mobile app, Microsoft OneDrive, and Outlook email.
    

### Test Case 3: The Broken Local Business (Hobbyist Stack, High Friction)

_Use this to test how your "Free Tier" handles a completely unoptimized business running entirely on spreadsheets._

- **Industry:** Local Residential Landscaping & Hardscaping
    
- **Business Size:** 4 field crew members / $450k annual revenue
    
- **Ecosystem Platforms Q1:** We just use Google Sheets, Excel, personal Gmail accounts, and Venmo/Zelle for payments. Nothing talks.
    
- **Admin Friction & Risk Q2:** Copying lead information out of website contact emails and manually typing them into a master Google Sheet tracker. If we don't do it instantly, we lose track of the client and a competitor books them.
    
- **Primary Bottleneck & Hours Q3:** Writing out paper estimates at night after working in the field all day. I easily waste 10 hours a week sitting at a desk doing paperwork instead of pricing new jobs.
    
- **Knowledge Base State Q4:** Entirely unwritten employee memory. Everything about how to mix materials or set up specific irrigation cycles lives inside my own head.
    
- **Information Synthesis Tax Q5:** When a client calls asking why their monthly invoice doesn't match their original quote, I have to go through my sent email history, pull up the original estimate spreadsheet, and check my bank app to see what they already paid.
    
- **Conditional Logic Rules Q6:** Calculating project deposits: If a project quote is over $5,000 or requires ordering custom stone, require a 50% down payment before scheduling. If it's under $5,000 for standard maintenance, just bill them upon completion.
    
- **Multi-App Loop Platforms Q7:** Yes: Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Square Invoicing.
