"I've started doing AI assessments for business owners. I'm doing one free one this month to sharpen the process. Want it?"

Three rules before you send anything:





One offer per message. Free assessment first. Paid comes after you have proof.



Log every send in your Warm Outreach Tracker. If it's not logged, it didn't happen.



Reply speed beats perfection. A fast, imperfect reply wins.

Awesome. It's a 30-minute call where I ask about how your business runs, then I send you a short report with your top AI opportunities. Zero pitch, you keep the report either way. Does [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] work?

4. Phone Call Talk Track

Call Hot contacts only. Everyone else gets text or DM first.

Thirty-minute call, I ask how your business runs, then I put together a report showing exactly where AI can save you time or make you money. Then immediately text them the Text Message script above. The voicemail warms it, the text books it.

5. In-Person Ask (gym, church, chamber, meetup)

Don't pitch mid-conversation. Plant it, then follow up on their phone.



Plant: [When business comes up naturally] "Actually, I just started doing AI assessments for business owners. I find where AI saves them time or makes them money. I'm doing a free one this month. You'd honestly be a great fit for it."

If interested: "Let me text you the details so it's easy." [Get the number, send the Text Message script that night.]



PART 2: THE RECONNECT MESSAGE

For people you haven't talked to in a year or more. Reconnect first, offer second. Never lead with the ask.

Message 1 (no offer):



[Name]! It's been way too long. Saw [the thing: their post, their business news, a mutual friend mentioned them] and it made me think of you. How's [business/family/the thing you knew about them] going?

Message 2 (after they reply, in the same conversation or next day):



Love hearing that. On my end, big change, I've moved into AI consulting. I do AI assessments for business owners: 30-minute call, then a report showing where AI saves you time or makes you money. I'm doing one free one this month to sharpen the process. Given what you just told me about [thing they mentioned], you'd honestly be a perfect fit. Want it?
