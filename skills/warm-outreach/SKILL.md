# Warm Outreach

Source scripts: [[Warm Outreach Scripts]]. Use them as the base for any warm outreach message, call track, or follow-up.

1. Pick the part that fits the contact:
   - Hot/Warm, in recent contact → Part 1 (text, DM, email, call, in-person). Phone calls are for Hot contacts only.
   - Not talked in a year+ → Part 2 reconnect. Message 1 never includes the offer.
   - Free assessment delivered + testimonial in hand → Part 3 flip to paid ($999).
   - Liked/commented on a proof post → Part 4 engagement DM, same day.
2. Keep the core one-liner intact: "I've started doing AI assessments for business owners. I'm doing one free one this month to sharpen the process. Want it?"
3. Rules: one offer per message; free first, paid only after proof; fast and imperfect beats polished.
4. Fill every `[bracket]` from what's known about the contact (see [[mirk-sales-and-outreach]] warm lists). Cut anything that doesn't sound like Ian.
5. Never invent a testimonial or client result. If none exists yet, use Part 1, not Parts 3–4.
6. Remind Ian to log every send in the Warm Outreach Tracker.
