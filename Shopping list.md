This grocery list is designed to transition you away from a "lettuce-only" diet toward one packed with antioxidants, healthy fats, and high-impact vitamins.
These quantities are for **one person for one week**, assuming you eat a variety of these daily.
### 1. The "Daily Powerhouses" Grocery List
| Category | Item | Quantity | Why? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Greens** | **Organic Baby Spinach** | 1 large bag (5-10oz) | Replaces lettuce; 3x the nutrients. |
| **Berries** | **Blueberries or Raspberries** | 3-4 half-pint containers | Your daily antioxidant "medicine." |
| **Crunch** | **Honeycrisp Apples** | 5-7 large apples | High-fiber snack to replace processed food. |
| **Vegetable** | **Cherry Tomatoes** | 2-3 pint containers | High in Lycopene; easy daily snack. |
| **The "Sweet"** | **Medjool Dates** | 1 small container | Natural energy; eat 2 per day. |
| **Healthy Fat** | **Raw Almonds** | 1 bag (8-10oz) | Pairs with dates and apples to stabilize sugar. |
| **Healthy Fat** | **Walnuts** | 1 bag (8oz) | Critical for Omega-3s and brain health. |
| **Cruciferous** | **Broccoli Florets** | 1 large pre-cut bag | Contains sulforaphane for detox. |
| **Vitamins** | **Red Bell Peppers** | 3 large peppers | Massive Vitamin C boost for skin/immunity. |
| **Cooking Oil** | **Avocado Oil** | 1 bottle | High smoke point; heart-healthy monounsaturated fat. |
### 2. How to "Eat" Your Groceries (The Weekly Strategy)
 * **The "Base" Swap:** Use the **Spinach** for every meal where you previously used lettuce. If you miss the crunch, mix in sliced **Bell Peppers**.
 * **The 3 PM Energy Boost:** Pit **2 Medjool dates** and stuff them with an **almond** or two. This is your "healthy candy bar."
 * **The TV Snack:** Instead of chips, put a bowl of **Cherry Tomatoes** and **Walnuts** on the table.
 * **The Breakfast Hack:** Throw a handful of **Blueberries** into your morning routine. If you have oatmeal or yogurt, the berries act as the sweetener so you don't need sugar or honey.
### 3. A Note on Freshness
 * **Apples and Almonds:** These will last all month.
 * **Spinach and Berries:** These are the most sensitive. Eat these in the first 4-5 days of the week.
 * **Tomatoes:** Keep them on the counter, not the fridge! They taste much better and retain their texture at room temperature.
