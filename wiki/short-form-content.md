# Short-Form Content & Digital Products

Notes from a 4/20 workshop on short-form video and selling a $97 digital product through HighLevel ([[4-20 workshop]]).

- Retention first: watch time drives distribution. Hide the idea and use "you're doing it wrong" / "look at this" hooks.
- Package existing knowledge as a $97 product (it outperforms $47). Deliver it via Google Docs.
- Pull people off-platform to capture email (not Linktree), then send a welcome email and sell while they're engaged. Read "1,000 True Fans".
- Facebook ads to a GHL landing page work; Ikigai for picking a niche; HeyGen avatars exist, but a real face wins long term.
