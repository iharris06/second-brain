# MIRK Offers & Pricing

The offer ladder is: audit to get in the door, then a monthly automation plan, then AI agents and team-wide audits. Prices differ between notes; see Open questions.

## Ladder (as written in the sources)
| Offer | Price | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials bundle | $197–297/mo | Missed-call text-back + review booster in GHL; upsell to AI agents in week 4 | [[Agency Creation Checklist]] |
| Starter | $497/mo | Core automation plan, 30-day money-back guarantee as the close tool | [[7 Day Plan]] |
| Business X-Ray audit | Free (anchored at $997) | Door-opener during the 7-day sprint | [[7 Day Plan]] |
| AI assessment | $999 | Goal is 5/month = ~$5k before retainers | [[Money Math]] |
| Deep Dive | $1,000 | 30-min call → transcript → Claude → interactive [[ai-audit-process\|Business X-Ray]] or PDF proposal | [[Xray prompt]], [[Ai Audit Call flow]] |
| Team audit | $3,000–5,000 | Survey 15+ employees, synthesize with Claude/NotebookLM, 1:1 interviews with dept heads | [[$3000 package]], [[$3000 notebookllm]] |

Checkout plans tagged in the CRM: starter, professional, hosting, voice-standard, voice-pro, deep-dive ([[MIRK CRM Tags]]). Professional, hosting and the voice tiers have no price written down anywhere in the vault.

Post-assessment promise: a report with quick wins and longer-term projects, which the client can DIY or hire out, plus a 2-week follow-up ([[After the assessment]]).
