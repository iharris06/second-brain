# Day Job: O3 1:1s & Career Levels

Notes from recurring 1:1s (O3s) at Ian's consulting employer (Publicis Sapient).

## Level ladder ([[2025-07-11 - Promotion and Levels]], [[2026-03-06]])
- Associate L1 → L2 → Senior Associate L1 → L2 → Architect → Senior Architect (spans accounts) → Director (BU leadership).
- Senior Associate L2 means owning quality for the team: code reviews, design docs, standards, tests.
- To get to L2: spot the patterns used across the project, think ahead about growth, use AI (coding assistants, design review, automation), and build a promotion story with concrete goals. No set time in role; standard raise is 8–15%.
