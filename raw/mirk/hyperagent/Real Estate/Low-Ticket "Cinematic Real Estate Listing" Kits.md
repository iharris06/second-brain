# Summary
Real estate agents are constantly hunting for cost-effective ways to make their listings pop on social media. Hyperagent excels at turning a messy real estate URL into a structured listing package.
## Details

- **The Offer:** "The 24-Hour Premium Listing Kit." Includes an editorial single-property landing page, a cinematic property video short, a buyer’s handout PDF, and five social media promotional scripts.
    
- **How the Agent Runs:** The agent uses Hyperagent’s sandboxed browser to scrape a standard Redfin, Zillow, or MLS listing page. It extracts the property features, maps out the neighborhood context, processes the listing images through its internal video/slide generator engine, and outputs a highly polished, formatted media kit.
    
- **The Velocity Strategy:** Target newly active real estate listings in mid-tier brackets ($400k–$700k) where agents don't have the budget for a $1,000 professional videographer but desperately need to stand out. Sell the entire turnkey kit for a flat **$99 to $149**. You can fulfill 20 of these a day with a single agent thread.
