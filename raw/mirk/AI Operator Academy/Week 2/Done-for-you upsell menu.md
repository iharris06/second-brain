The Concierge retainer is done-with-you. You build alongside the client, they keep the skills, and the engagement stays light. Around month one or two, almost every client says: "Can you just do this one for me?" That sentence is the upsell menu opening itself.

The base engagement is the anchor. Everything on the menu sits on top of the Concierge retainer at $1,000 to $2,000 a month. Never replace the retainer with a project: the retainer is the relationship, where you find the projects, scope them on strategy calls, and stay accountable through the Notion hub. The menu is for work the client wants done for them, outside the working sessions.

Menu, entry to flagship:
- Automation Build, $1,000–3,000. Working automation in Zapier or Make.com that eliminates the manual steps the audit identified. Deliverable: the automation, a walkthrough video, a one-page reference doc.
- Process Redesign, $3,000–5,000. Map one core process end to end, identify waste, redesign for AI. Deliverable: documented process with before/after states, human/AI handoff points, prioritized implementation roadmap. AOA productized.
- Knowledge Systems, $3,000+. Custom GPT or internal knowledge base trained on the client's SOPs, FAQs, institutional knowledge.
- Custom Workflows, $3,000–5,000. Toolkit of prompts, templates, one-click operations for the client's daily work ("build me a library of skills for my whole team").
- Full Implementation, $5,000–10,000+. Custom AI agents, full workflow automation, system integration, ongoing support. Rule: don't offer until 5–10 lower-tier projects are delivered.

How the upsell happens: don't pitch cold. Strategy calls produce action items; some are too big for a 45-minute session. Line: "We can chip away at this across our next few calls, or I can take it off your plate as a project. Here's what that looks like." The Notion hub (quantified build list) does the selling; buying a project is a reorder.

Math: 6 retainer clients at $1,500–2,000 = $9,000–12,000/mo recurring. 1–2 menu projects/month add $2,000–10,000 from existing clients, with zero new acquisition.
