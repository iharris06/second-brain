### Checkout Status Tags

These apply to all plans and are perfect for abandoned cart or post-purchase workflows:

- `checkout-initiated` (Added when Step 1 is submitted)
- `checkout-completed` (Added when Step 2 is submitted)

### Plan Interest Tags (Added on Step 1)

Use these to know exactly what plan they abandoned or are interested in:

- `plan-starter`
- `plan-professional`
- `plan-hosting`
- `plan-voice-standard`
- `plan-voice-pro`
- `plan-deep-dive`

### Purchase Confirmation Tags (Added on Step 2)

Use these to trigger specific onboarding emails or access based on what they bought:

- `purchased-starter`
- `purchased-professional`
- `purchased-hosting`
- `purchased-voice-standard`
- `purchased-voice-pro`
- `purchased-deep-dive`
