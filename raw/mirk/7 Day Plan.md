 7-Day Blueprint: First Paying MIRK Client

Strategy: Warm network + free Business X-Ray audit as the door-opener → convert to Starter ($497/mo). Volume covers for close-rate uncertainty. Run both tracks in parallel, not sequentially.

Day 1 (Mon) — Build the pipeline

- Warm list: 15-20 people in your network who own or know home service business owners (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, garage door, roofing). Text/DM them directly — don’t wait for the Reel to work alone.
- Cold list: 30 home service businesses in Mesa/East Valley with visible pain signals — bad or slow-to-respond Google reviews, no online booking, voicemail-only phone tree. Google Maps + GBP reviews is enough; skip fancy tooling.
- Post the Instagram Reel (3 free audits, scarcity, “DM AUDIT”).

Day 2 (Tue) — Outreach

- Send all warm DMs/texts personally, not the Reel script — 1:1 messages convert better than broadcast.
- Cold outreach: call or walk in, don’t email-only. Home service owners don’t read email. Script: “I noticed [specific thing — slow review response / no after-hours booking]. I do a free audit showing exactly what missed calls are costing you. Takes 10 minutes, no pitch.”
- Goal: 5-8 confirmed audit slots by end of day.

Day 3 (Wed) — Deliver audits

- Use the Business X-Ray HTML template, personalized per prospect (their actual review gaps, estimated missed-call volume, rough $ lost/month). Personalization is what makes the $997 anchor believable even though it’s free this week.
- Send/present same-day if possible — momentum matters more than polish right now.

Day 4 (Thu) — Walk-through calls

- 15-20 min call per prospect: walk the audit, quantify the leak (“X missed calls/mo × your average job value = $Y lost”), then pitch Starter ($497/mo) as the fix.
- Close in the room where you can. Don’t send a proposal and wait — the 30-day money-back guarantee is your close tool, use it explicitly: “If it’s not paying for itself in 30 days, you don’t pay.”

Day 5 (Fri) — Objection handling + close

- Follow up everyone from Thu who didn’t close. Most “yes” comes on the 2nd touch, not the 1st.
- For hesitant ones: offer to set up just the missed-call text-back piece first (smallest visible win) rather than the full stack — lower the commitment, not the price.

Day 6-7 (Weekend) — Onboarding + refill pipeline

- Contract + first invoice for whoever closed. Get their GHL setup started immediately — a client using it by Monday is worth more than the signature itself for testimonials/case study.
- If no close yet: this is your second outreach wave. Don’t stop the cold list — go back to Day 1’s 30 with the audits from people who already got them, showing social proof (“just did this for [similar business type] down the street”).

The one thing that matters most

Volume of conversations, not polish of materials. Your audit deliverable and pricing are already dialed in — the bottleneck this week is just getting in front of enough owners. Prioritize warm network first (it’ll close fastest), cold walk-ins/calls second, and don’t let the Reel be your only lead source — it’s the slowest of the three.
