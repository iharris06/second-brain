## What to Do with the Red Potatoes (The "Healthy" Hack)
You mentioned having red potatoes but not knowing how to use them healthily. Potatoes are actually an *incredible* tool for bodybuilding and fat loss because they rank the absolute highest on the human "Satiety Index" (they keep you full longer than almost any other food).
Here is the healthiest, easiest way to prepare them with your current inventory:
### Spiced Air-Fryer (or Baked) Red Potato Wedges
 1. **Prep:** Wash the red potatoes (leave the skins on—that's where the fiber and potassium sit) and cut them into small wedges or cubes.
 2. **Coat:** Toss them in a large bowl with just **1 teaspoon of avocado oil** (a little goes a long way to keep calories down).
 3. **Season:** Heavy-season them with your powdered spices: garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
 4. **Cook:** Air-fry at 400°F (200°C) for 15–20 minutes (or bake at 425°F for 25 mins), shaking the basket halfway through until they are crispy on the outside and soft on the inside.
**How to use them:** Swap these in place of rice or quinoa for lunch or dinner. Weigh out about 150g–200g of these cooked potatoes—it will look like a massive, highly satisfying plate of food for very few calories, keeping your energy levels high for those heavy kettlebell presses.
